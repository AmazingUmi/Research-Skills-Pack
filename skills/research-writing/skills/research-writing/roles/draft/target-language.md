# Drafting or polishing in the target language

Write the requested manuscript text in the chosen language. For a bilingual deliverable, apply the appropriate language guidance to each requested version. Keep the author's established terminology, citation keys, symbols, numerical precision and units in sync across versions.

For Chinese text, follow the manuscript's simplified or traditional script and its conventions for names and abbreviations. Preserve which observation, inference or condition each clause describes. When polishing Chinese prose, keep its meaning and edit only within the requested scope. Give any revision notes in the author's requested language.

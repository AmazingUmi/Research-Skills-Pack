# Nature editorial criteria and processes

This pack includes a brief working summary. Consult the publisher's page for
current criteria and exact wording. [Nature's editorial criteria and processes](https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/editorial-criteria-and-processes)

- For a Nature Article, assess research originality, scientific significance,
  and whether the findings matter across specialties.
- Editorial screening weighs the novelty, implications, and accessibility of
  the research. Technical review examines the evidence and methods in depth.
- Referees explain which readers would benefit from the results and identify
  technical concerns that affect the claims. Editors make publication decisions.

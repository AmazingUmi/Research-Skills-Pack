Paper Orchestra Skills v0.2.0

These local Codex skills and their reference files are distributed under the
Apache License, Version 2.0. They adapt the workflow and roles described in
PaperOrchestra by Yiwen Song, Yale Song, Tomas Pfister, and Jinsung Yoon.

Upstream: https://github.com/google-research/paper-orchestra
Reference commit: d9bae997e793a015ed38bb1605be9eb58c00284f
Upstream paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05018

The local adaptation implements the workflow as six instructions for Codex,
with claim-to-evidence handoffs and routing to Writing specialist packs.
The accompanying LICENSE reproduces the license at the upstream reference
commit.
